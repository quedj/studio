# **App Name**: QUE Digital & Rhythm

## Core Features:

- Service Discovery Dashboard: A high-impact split interface allowing users to navigate between the 'Marketplace App Development' and 'Professional DJ Services' divisions with distinct visual themes.
- AI Vibe & Tech Architect Tool: A generative tool where clients describe their event or business vision to receive an AI-suggested project roadmap for their app or a customized setlist energy profile for their event.
- Interactive Booking Portal: A unified inquiry system with dynamic form fields that adapt based on whether a client is seeking a software development partnership or a DJ performance booking.
- Development Lifecycle Visualization: A stylized timeline showing the journey from initial marketplace architecture to the finished peer-to-peer product.
- Real-time Inquiry Confirmation: Automated professional receipt of project briefs and booking requests for streamlined client onboarding.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: Bold Red (#E60000) extracted directly from the logo's 'u' and 'j' elements to represent energy and passion.
- Background Color: Solid Black (#000000) to match the primary branding and provide a sophisticated, high-contrast digital environment.
- Accent Color: High-contrast White (#FFFFFF) for text and UI borders, mimicking the clean 'EXPERIENCE' block in the logo.
- Headline font: 'Poppins' for a geometric, modern look that complements the bold, circular shapes in the logo. Body font: 'Inter' for maximum readability.
- Minimalist Lucide icons utilizing a duo-tone Red and White style to maintain brand consistency.
- A clean, responsive split-column Hero section that balances the professional development side with the creative entertainment side.
- Subtle rhythmic pulse animations on primary call-to-action buttons, timed to a 120BPM tempo to simulate a musical beat.
